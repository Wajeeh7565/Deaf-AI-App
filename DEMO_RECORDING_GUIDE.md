# Short airport demo recording

Target length: **25–35 seconds**, portrait/mobile view.

Recommended sequence:

1. **Welcome (2–3 s):** official YKF branding → choose **LSQ**.
2. **Flight selection (6–8 s):** show sticky interpreter, official airline logos and YKF kiosk photos → select Calgary/WestJet.
3. **Confirmation (2–3 s):** show airline logo, destination, time and gate → Confirm and continue.
4. **Baggage/security (5–7 s):** briefly show the YKF visual landmark/photo and LSQ guidance.
5. **CATSA (5–7 s):** show the interpreter + security photo and step navigation.
6. **Gate (4–5 s):** show large gate number, airline logo and official YKF gate photo.

Recording notes:

- Keep the phone in portrait orientation.
- Do not linger on demo/testing controls.
- Use LSQ because the processed LSQ clips are available now.
- Leave the **Demo data** chip visible so nobody mistakes sample flights for a live airport feed.
- Do not use the gate-change 5.4 scenario in the main recording until its video is processed to match the new face-swapped set.
